This policy explains when course fees paid to **Feed My Brain** can be refunded. By paying for a course, you agree to this policy.

## 1. No refunds once the course has started

Once your batch has started, meaning your first live class has taken place, **course fees are non-refundable**. This applies whether or not you attend the classes.

## 2. Emergency refunds before your first class

We understand that genuine emergencies happen. A refund may be considered **only if all of the following are true**:

- You have paid the course fee, **and**
- Your first live class has **not** yet taken place, **and**
- You are unable to join because of a **genuine emergency**, such as a medical emergency, a family bereavement or another serious, unforeseen situation.

Refund requests are reviewed case by case, and Feed My Brain may ask for reasonable supporting information. Approval of a refund is at the discretion of Feed My Brain.

## 3. How to request a refund

Send your request **before your first class** to:

- Email: [feedmybrain211@gmail.com](mailto:feedmybrain211@gmail.com)
- WhatsApp: [+91 90927 30955](https://wa.me/919092730955)

Please include your full name, the course and batch you enrolled in, your payment details (date, amount and transaction reference) and a short description of the emergency.

## 4. Processing

- We will respond to your request within **7 working days**.
- Approved refunds are paid back to the **original payment method** (or by bank transfer / UPI if the original method is not available), usually within **7–10 working days** of approval.
- Any payment-gateway or bank charges that we cannot recover may be deducted from the refund amount.

## 5. Batch transfer instead of refund

If you cannot attend your batch before it starts, you may ask to move to a later batch instead of a refund. Transfers are subject to seat availability and are at Feed My Brain's discretion.

## 6. Cancellation by Feed My Brain

If Feed My Brain cancels a batch before it starts and cannot offer you a suitable alternative batch, you will receive a **full refund** of the course fee.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page at the time of your payment applies to your enrollment.
